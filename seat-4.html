SEATStarting.

One sphere of directions, cut into triangles. Every triangle has an address such as f07·2103: face 07, then one digit for each level down.

An eye looks around and leaves what it sees in the triangles it covers. Where it has looked more often, the triangles are smaller.

Solid colour was seen. Screened colour is inferred from neighbours. Blank was never reached.

Pinch or scroll to zoom. Drag to turn or pan. Tap a triangle to read it. Double-tap it, or press ENTER, to go into it: the view flies there and the eye looks there, so detail builds where you go. OUT steps back one level.

After FILM finishes, press SAVE FILM to keep the video.

Shots are made in INSIDE. The rectangle is the film frame. Aim and zoom, press MARK IN, aim and zoom again, press MARK OUT. That is one shot. PLAY runs the shots in order with cuts between them. FILM records them as a video with sound and saves it. Once there are shots, the red outlines mean: this region is in frame and is not yet fine enough for that lens.

ORB shows the sphere from outside. INSIDE shows it from the centre. NET shows it unfolded. ROOM draws it on a floor and two walls so that it is correct from the seat.

SCREEN toggles the screening of inferred colour. SHORT toggles the red outlines. PIXEL is the size of one pixel on the real surface.

In ROOM, drag the seat to move it. Stand elsewhere to see the stretch. Pin a position to say that people will stand there too.

A red outline means a region needs finer triangles than it has, given this seat, these pins and this surface pixel size. The eye goes to red regions first.

Tap any triangle to read it and to leave a note on it. The tape slider reads the sphere as it was earlier.

The camera source needs an orientation sensor. It stops at depth 4, because the sensor is only good to a few degrees.
